Auto-mark test files as viewed

Automatically check the "Viewed" box on test, snapshot, and mock files in PR diffs so they stay out of your review flow.

Hide dependency PRs

Click the PruneR toolbar icon on a <owner>/<repo>/pulls page to toggle the -label:dependencies filter. When active, the icon shows a badge 12 with the number of open dependabot / dependency PRs currently hidden.
